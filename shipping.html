Shipping & Returns

Our commitment to getting your botanical rituals to you safely and sustainably.

Standard Shipping

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days. Shipping rates are calculated based on your location and will be shown at checkout. We prioritize plastic-free packaging and carbon-neutral transit partners where possible.

Returns & Exchanges

We want you to love your WeCare products. If you're not satisfied, we offer a 30-day return policy for unopened items in original packaging. Due to the botanical nature of our ingredients, opened products cannot be returned unless they are defective.

Damaged Items

If your order arrives damaged, please contact us within 48 hours of receipt with photographic evidence so we can arrange a swift replacement ritual.

Questions?

Contact our support team at hello@wecare.com for any inquiries regarding your shipment.
